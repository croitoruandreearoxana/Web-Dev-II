Small Shifts, Real Impact

Practical adjustments to reduce domestic energy consumption, minimize packaging waste, and establish sustainable habits that stick.

Core Living Principles

Energy

Smart Consumption

Unplug phantom loads, switch fixtures to low-draw LEDs, and program cooling or heating devices to conserve energy during empty hours.

Waste

Closed-Loop Kitchen

Separate organic trimmings for home compost, freeze leftover produce, and eliminate single-use wrappers by stocking bulk dry staples.

Transit

Low-Emission Travel

Combine local errands into linked trips, prioritize active cycling or transit, and replace short solo vehicle routes with daily walking.

Instant Practice Toggle

Home Checklist

Lower your water heater temperature to 49°C, clean refrigerator coils twice a year, and install weather stripping on draughty exterior doors.
